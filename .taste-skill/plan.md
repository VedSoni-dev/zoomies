features h2: What zoomies does to a recording. <muted>All of it in the tab, on Windows, ChromeOS, Linux or Mac.</muted>
 - Records a screen, window or tab / Pick what to share in the browser's picker. Your microphone is one switch.
 - Takes a recording from any app / Drop an MP4, MOV or WebM from QuickTime, OBS, Game Bar, Loom or a phone.
 - Frames it for X, LinkedIn or Reels / Nine backgrounds, a colour or your own image, at 16:9, 1:1 or 9:16.
 - Exports an MP4 at 1080p, 60 fps / Encoded in the tab with its sound. A 7.5 second clip took 5.3 seconds.
statement: Nothing leaves the tab / zoomies reads, zooms and encodes the video in your browser. Projects stay on this computer, in its own storage.
how h2: How zoomies places a zoom
 - Four times a second, zoomies compares each frame with the one before on a small copy.
 - facts: A small changed area becomes a zoom. / A scroll or page load changes the whole screen and never starts one. / Zooms close together merge; far ones pan.
